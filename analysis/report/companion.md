# Companion — What this report shows for the Senior Data Analyst, Marketing role

This report is a **community-support friction audit** (41k Discord threads, 2022–2026) — not marketing measurement. What it *does* demonstrate is the analytical practice the role is hiring for, applied to a different signal: detrending against a baseline to separate real movement from a shrinking corpus (the same move that separates incrementality from platform-reported numbers), NMF topic modeling on community language (the same decomposition that exposes theme-level contribution in paid-channel reporting), and reply-layer triage of 96 high-friction threads coded into root-cause / resolution / fix categories (the same readout discipline used in experiment analysis). Built end-to-end solo — pipeline, ML layer, self-serve artifact, palette validation, visual verification — and shipped as a self-serve tool so non-technical stakeholders (docs, product, support) can act on it without coming back. AI-forward throughout: parallel AI subagents drove the triage layer, NMF labeling, and friction-signal engineering. The data is different from what the role asks for; the discipline is the same.
